Privacy Policy

SpeakPerin LLC · Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: Perin has no ads and no third-party analytics or trackers. We never sell your data. Your voice recordings are transcribed and immediately discarded — we do not store audio. Your learning progress is saved to your account so it follows you across devices. The weekly board shows other learners only your first name and last initial, and you can turn that off.

1. What We Collect

Account data: your email and the name you provide, stored with Supabase. Learning data: progress, XP, session counts, and usage limits, stored in your Supabase profile. On a free account, today's free practice and your free Live conversation try are counted on your device. Synced learning data: your vocabulary, lesson and conversation history, completed scenarios, grammar notes, saved phrases, recurring-mistake patterns, profile, and language settings are stored on your device and backed up to your account in Supabase, so your progress syncs across devices. The focus your guide picks from your last conversation and your guide's notes are also backed up to your account. People you meet in scenes: after a scene, our AI may note up to two short things you said about yourself (for example, where you're from) so the same character can remember you next time. These notes are saved to your account and synced; you can see them and forget them on the Progress page, and we never keep health, money, religion, politics, relationships or other private details there. Weekly board: when you earn XP, our server records your weekly XP, the name shown on the board (your first name and last initial), and the language variety you practice most that week. Other learners in your weekly group can see that name, variety and XP. You can hide yourself anytime in Settings ("Show me on the weekly board"); deleting your account deletes these records. Display preferences (such as dark mode and accent color), practice reminder settings, your Cook with a local cookbook, and the chapter your Journey starts at stay on your device only. Practice reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; we do not use push notification services. Conversation text: what you type or say (as text) during sessions, processed to generate AI replies. Crash reports: if the app crashes, an error description, the page you were on, and your IP address are sent to our own server — never to a third-party analytics company — and deleted after 7 days. Security: our server uses your account ID and IP address to enforce usage limits and prevent abuse; these counters expire within days.

2. Your Voice

When you press the microphone, your audio is sent through our server to OpenAI's Whisper service, which converts it to text. We do not store your audio — not on our servers, not in our database. Only the resulting text is used in your session. OpenAI may retain API audio briefly (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring and does not use it to train their models.

3. AI Conversations

To generate replies, your conversation text is sent to Anthropic (our AI provider). To speak replies aloud, reply text is sent to ElevenLabs, which returns audio. These providers process your content to deliver the service and are bound by their own privacy commitments for API data. Your conversation history is saved to your account so you can review past sessions; deleting your account deletes it.

4. Payments

Web subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat and processed by Stripe; subscriptions purchased in our mobile apps are processed by Apple or Google. Your card details go directly to the payment processor; we never see or store your full card number. We receive only what we need to manage your subscription (such as its status).

5. Who We Share Data With

Only the service providers needed to run Perin: Supabase (accounts and data), Anthropic (AI replies), OpenAI (speech transcription), ElevenLabs (speech audio), RevenueCat (subscription management), Stripe (web payments), Apple and Google (app store purchases), and Cloudflare (hosting and routing). We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account in Settings, which erases your data from our systems, including your subscription record at RevenueCat. Apple, Google and Stripe keep their own billing records under their policies. Data stored on your device can be removed by clearing your browser data or deleting the app. For help with deletion or data requests, email us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email support@speakperin.com and we will respond promptly. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Children

Perin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn we have, we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. We will post updates here and notify you of material changes in the app or by email.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@speakperin.com.

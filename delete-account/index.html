Delete your Perin account

Perin: Language Learning · SpeakPerin LLC

You can delete your Perin account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app (iPhone, Android or speakperin.com)

  1. Sign in to Perin.
  2. Tap your profile (the circle with your initial, top right), then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

By email

If you can't sign in, email support@speakperin.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.

If you have Perin Pro

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it there first: on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. A subscription bought on speakperin.com has to be canceled in Settings before the account can be deleted.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account on our servers: your email and name, your learning progress and XP, saved words and phrases, conversation history, your guide's notes, what the people in your scenes remember about you, your weekly board entries, and your subscription record at RevenueCat. Nothing is kept afterwards.

What may be kept

Apple, Google and Stripe keep their own records of purchases under their own policies; we can't delete those. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 7 days. Data saved on your own phone or browser is removed when you delete the app or clear your browser data.

Questions: support@speakperin.com. More in our Privacy Policy.
